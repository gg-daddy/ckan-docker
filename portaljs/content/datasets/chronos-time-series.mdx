# Overview

Chronos datasets is a comprehensive collection of time series data designed for forecasting tasks. It includes both univariate and multivariate time series from various domains.

**Key Features:**
- 67 different subsets covering diverse domains
- Supports univariate and multivariate forecasting
- Over 10 million rows of time series data
- Used for training and evaluating Chronos foundation models

## Dataset Information

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Task** | Time Series Forecasting |
| **Modality** | Tabular, Time-series |
| **Size** | 10M - 100M rows |
| **Downloads** | 13K+ monthly |
| **Format** | parquet |
| **License** | Other |
